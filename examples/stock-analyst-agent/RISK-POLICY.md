# Risk policy (hard limits)

Defaults below are placeholders. The user must confirm or change them before Phase 3.

- Max single position: 15% of portfolio value
- Max new capital per order: 5% of portfolio value
- Max orders per day: 2; per week: 5
- Min cash reserve: 10%
- No margin, options, leverage, short selling, or penny stocks
- Only tickers on `research/notes/watchlist.md` with a written thesis
- Buy only with documented margin of safety (target: 25%+ below estimated intrinsic value)
- Review/exit trigger on every position: thesis broken, or -20% from cost basis (review, not auto-sell)
- Limit orders only; no market orders outside regular hours
- Halt all trading and notify the user if portfolio drawdown from peak exceeds 15%
